Why Lumina

A clinic that runs on systems, not queues

Founded in 2013 by Dr. Meera Rao, Lumina was built around one idea: healthcare should feel calm. Appointments start on time, quotes are written and all-inclusive, and every treatment is explained before it begins.

Our MG Road flagship and North Branch share one digital record — so your history, scans and reminders follow you, not the other way around.
