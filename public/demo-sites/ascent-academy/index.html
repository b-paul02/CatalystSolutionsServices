Admissions 2026–27

Four simple steps from enquiry to enrolment

Most families complete the process within two weeks. Our counselors guide you at every step — no agents, no hidden fees.

  1. Step 01

    Enquire

    Share your child's age and stage through the form below or a quick call. A counselor responds within one working day.

  2. Step 02

    Visit the campus

    A guided walk-through with your child — classrooms, labs, sports fields — plus time with the stage coordinator.

  3. Step 03

    Interaction & assessment

    An age-appropriate, low-pressure interaction — informal for primary years, a short written assessment from Class VI.

  4. Step 04

    Enrol

    Receive the offer, complete documentation and fee formalities online, and join the orientation week in March.
