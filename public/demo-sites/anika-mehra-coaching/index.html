How we work together

A journey with a shape, not a subscription

Coaching that drifts is coaching that fails. Every engagement follows the same three-movement arc — diagnostic, work, integration.

01

Discovery call

30 minutes · Free

A genuinely diagnostic conversation about where you are and what the next chapter demands. If coaching is not the right tool, Anika will say so and point you somewhere better.

02

The 90-day arc

Repeated twice per engagement

We name one leadership edge that actually matters — the board, the team, the founder identity — and work it in fortnightly sessions with async support between, against real situations, in real time.

03

Integration

Final month

We consolidate what changed into practices you own without a coach: a forward plan, the habits that held, and a clear-eyed review with the stakeholders who watched you grow.
