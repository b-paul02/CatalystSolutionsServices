The Verve promise

Fewer ingredients. Better sourcing. No guesswork.

Every formula is made in small batches in our Mumbai lab and shipped fresh — never warehoused for a year.

Since 2019

Started at a Bandra farmers' market. Still made the same way.

Verve began as a single face oil blended in founder Riya Kapoor's kitchen and sold at the Linking Road farmers' market. The formula hasn't changed — only the batch size has.

Today a team of eleven — including two cosmetic chemists — makes every product in our own Mumbai lab. We bottle in weekly small batches, so what reaches you was made days ago, not months.

We publish every batch date, every supplier, and every third-party test. If we wouldn't put it on our own skin, it doesn't ship.

Ingredients & ethos

What's in the bottle — and what will never be

Our whole ingredient philosophy fits on a postcard. Here it is.

100% Vegan Cruelty-free Small-batch made No synthetic fragrance Recyclable glass

We name our farms

Rosehip from the Sharma orchards in Kullu. Jojoba from a women-run cooperative in Jaisalmer. Every supplier is listed with every product.

We publish our tests

Third-party heavy-metal and stability reports for every batch, downloadable from the batch code on your bottle.

We skip the filler

No synthetic fragrance, no drying alcohols, no dyes. If an ingredient is there for the smell or the shelf photo, it's not in ours.
