FAQ

Fair questions, straight answers

What beta teams ask us before connecting their feedback firehose.

How is this different from a spreadsheet with tags?

The clustering is automatic and it links every piece of feedback to the revenue behind it. Your spreadsheet can't tell you which complaint costs you $40k a year — Loopwise leads with that number.

When do I get access?

Waitlist invites go out every week, prioritised by feedback volume — the messier your current setup, the sooner you're in. Early users get founding pricing locked for life.

Which tools does it connect to?

The major support desks, CRMs, app-store review feeds, and survey tools — plus a CSV importer and an API for anything else. Setup is one click per channel, about five minutes total.

Is my customer data safe?

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, we never train on your content, and deletion requests propagate within 24 hours. SOC 2 Type I audit is in progress with a report expected this year.

What happens when the beta ends?

Free-beta teams keep a free tier, and anyone who upgraded during beta keeps the $29 founding price permanently. No surprise migrations, no forced upgrade emails.
