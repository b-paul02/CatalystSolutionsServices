How onboarding works

Live in 30 days. Quieter by day 90.

No rip-and-replace chaos. A sequenced transition that your team barely notices — except the tickets that stop arriving.

We inventory every endpoint, map your network and mine six months of ticket history to find what's actually generating the noise. You receive a written findings report — yours to keep either way.

Stabilize & sequence

Monitoring agents deploy, backups get verified, security baselines lock in. Then we sequence the fix plan noisiest-first, so your team feels the difference in the first month.
