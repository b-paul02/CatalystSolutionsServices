The firm

A quieter way to practise law

Founded in 2009 by two partners who left larger chambers, Meridian was built on a simple premise: clients deserve to know what their matter will cost, who is actually working on it, and what a realistic outcome looks like.

  • Written fee estimate before any work begins — fixed-fee phases wherever the matter allows.
  • Every matter is partner-led. Juniors assist; they never carry your file alone.
  • We will tell you when not to litigate — 92% of our matters conclude before trial.
  • Plain-language advice memos after every consultation, in English, Hindi or Marathi.

How an engagement works

Three steps, no surprises

Most clients tell us the anxiety of hiring a lawyer is not the law — it is the uncertainty. So we removed it.

  1. Step one

    Structured 30-minute consultation

    A fixed-fee session with the relevant partner. You send documents beforehand; the partner reads them first, so the full 30 minutes goes on advice — not intake paperwork.

    Credited against your matter if you instruct us.

  2. Step two

    Written strategy memo

    Within 48 hours you receive a plain-language memo: your position, realistic outcomes, the routes available, and a fee estimate for each. Yours to keep, whoever you hire.

    Including the honest option of doing nothing.

  3. Step three

    Partner-led matter

    If you proceed, the same partner runs your matter end to end — fixed-fee phases where possible, fortnightly written updates, and email answered within one working day.

    One partner, one file, start to finish.
