What we do

Four crews. Four specialities. One standard.

Every job — from a single flashing repair to a full commercial re-roof — gets the same written scope, the same site supervisor, and the same magnetic nail sweep before we leave.

Asphalt Shingle Roofing

Class 4 impact-rated architectural shingles installed to manufacturer spec — full tear-off, deck inspection, ice & water shield and ridge venting on every replacement.

Most homes done in 1–2 days

Metal & Standing Seam

Standing-seam and stone-coated steel systems rated for 140-mph wind and Colorado hail. A 40–70 year roof that sheds snow and cuts summer cooling loads.

50-yr manufacturer warranties

Storm Damage Restoration

Same-day tarping and emergency dry-in after hail or wind. We photograph, measure and document every slope so your insurance claim is adjuster-ready.

Same-day emergency response

Gutters & Drainage

Seamless 5" and 6" aluminum gutters, run on-site to length, with leaf protection and downspout routing that moves water away from your foundation.

Installed with every re-roof or solo

Why Summit Ridge

Seventeen years on Denver rooftops. Zero shortcuts.

Founded in 2009 by brothers Cole and Marcus Whitfield after one hail season too many spent fixing other contractors' work. Today our four in-house crews — no subcontracted labor — put on more than 300 roofs a year across the metro.

  • In-house crews only. The people on your roof are our employees — background-checked, harness-trained, supervised.
  • Everything in writing. Itemised scope, fixed price held 30 days, and a 15-year workmanship warranty on paper — not a handshake.
  • 24-hour estimates. Inspection today, written estimate in your inbox tomorrow. If we're late, we take $250 off the job.
  • Clean-site guarantee. Magnetic nail sweep of your lawn and driveway — twice — before the crew truck leaves.
