What we do

Full service, plainly delivered

Residential sales

Buyer representation

Commercial & investment

Market valuations

Why Harborview

A small firm, on purpose

Harborview opened in 2009 with two agents and one rule: return every call the same day. Seventeen years later we are still deliberately small — twelve agents across two offices — because the alternative is a brokerage where your listing is one of four hundred.

Every client works directly with a senior agent, not a team assistant. Every valuation is written by the person who will negotiate for you. It is slower to scale. It is better to work with.
