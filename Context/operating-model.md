# Catalyst Operating Model (Context)

Source: Customer Experience & Service Delivery Workflow v1.0 (July 2026). This is the target operating model the SaaS must fit into.

## Operating principles
1. **Diagnose before pitching** — discovery exists to understand the business, not sell a package.
2. **Capture evidence, with consent** — structured notes; recording/transcription only with explicit consent.
3. **AI analyzes, humans validate** — no AI-generated analysis or recommendation reaches a client without named human sign-off. Absolute rule.
4. **Prioritize by impact and effort** — quick wins first; every recommendation carries expected impact and cost of effort.
5. **Present live, work in the open** — recommendations reviewed live; shared client hub holds scope, status, decisions, next steps.
6. **Expand on evidence, not pressure** — renewals/upsells only from demonstrated value.

Positioning statement (used verbatim client-facing): "Every engagement is scoped and priced after discovery. The commercial model is selected according to the certainty of the scope, the type of work, the required team, and the expected duration."

## Customer lifecycle: 25 stages, 5 phases, 7 gates
1. **Attract & Qualify** — inquiry → acknowledgement → qualification call → **G1 Qualified opportunity** (growth lead approves; checklist: fit, problem in client's words, urgency, budget context as range never a quote, decision makers, red flags)
2. **Discover & Design** — discovery prep → structured session (12 areas) → AI-assisted analysis → mandatory human validation → **G2 Discovery complete** → solution + commercial model selection + estimate → **G3 Estimate approved** → recommendation presented live as the **Catalyst Solution Blueprint**
3. **Contract** — proposal/SOW/legal docs → negotiation → e-signature → activation → **G4 Contract active**
4. **Deliver** — onboarding + internal handoff + kickoff → **G5 Kickoff ready** → milestone/sprint execution, reporting, RAID, change control → QA/UAT/acceptance → **G6 Milestone accepted**
5. **Grow** — success reviews, value tracking → **G7 Renew or expand** → referral/case study/re-engagement

Single point of contact: growth lead until contract signature, then account manager (documented handoff).

## The 12 discovery areas
Business objective & desired outcome · users, customers & stakeholders · current situation & pain points · process, tools, tech, data & content · performance baseline · required skills & specialist roles · dependencies, integrations & access · scope, deliverables & priorities · timeline, urgency, budget context & procurement · compliance, privacy, security & brand constraints · definition of success & acceptance criteria · ongoing support & maintenance expectations.

## The Catalyst Solution Blueprint (11 sections)
Executive summary · business needs & root causes · recommended service workstreams · quick wins · impact-vs-effort matrix · proposed phases & roadmap · team & skills required · recommended commercial model · assumptions, exclusions & risks · measurement plan · next decision required. Ends with ONE clear decision, never a menu of twenty options.

Impact-vs-effort matrix: Quick wins (high impact, low effort — do first) · Major projects (high/high — plan and phase) · Fill-ins (low/low — spare capacity) · Park (low impact, high effort — defer openly).

Phased roadmap pattern: Phase 1 quick wins → Phase 2 core build → Phase 3 optimize & scale. First 30 days always made concrete.

## Three commercial models + decision tree
- Q1: Scope stable, bounded, clear acceptance criteria? YES → **Task-based, fixed scope** (audits, strategy work, websites, landing pages, brand identity, content batches, dashboards, automation builds, releases)
- Q2 (else): Ongoing capacity, changing priorities, recurring optimization? YES → **Managed capacity** (ongoing dev, optimization, maintenance, marketing ops, SEO, paid media, social, content production)
- Q3 (else): Continuous operations + defined projects combined? YES → **Hybrid** (base capacity/recurring fee + separately scoped tasks; double-billing guard)
- Still uncertain → recommend a paid/separately approved discovery & scoping phase first.

Likely model by family (● most common, ○ alternative): Consulting ●fixed ○hybrid · Website ●fixed ○hybrid · SEO ●capacity ○hybrid · Paid ads ●capacity ○hybrid · Social ●capacity ○hybrid · Content ○capacity ●fixed · Branding ●fixed · AI automation ○○●hybrid · App/software ○○●hybrid · Ecommerce ○capacity ●hybrid · Data ○fixed ●hybrid · Reputation/local ●capacity ○fixed · Retainers ●capacity ○hybrid.

## Rules that bind the SaaS
- No pricing before discovery — indicative effort levels only in any pre-discovery output.
- No AI output to a user without a named human reviewer's sign-off.
- Fit/urgency/budget-context/decision-maker = the G1 qualification dimensions.
- Internal estimating logic, rates, and margins never appear in anything client-facing.
- The internal post-analysis artifact follows the Part 4 schema (see questionnaire.md) so tool-originated and interview-originated leads look identical in review.
- Re-engagement track exists: declined/nurture leads are recorded, never discarded.
