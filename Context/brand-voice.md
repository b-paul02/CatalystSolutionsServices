# Catalyst Brand Voice (Context)

Identity: **Human-Led, AI-Enabled.** The voice must prove the "human-led" half. If a sentence sounds like it came from a template or a model, it has failed regardless of accuracy.

## Voice rules
1. Short sentences. One idea each. If a sentence needs two commas, split it.
2. Plain words over impressive ones. "Use" not "leverage". "Improve" not "elevate". "Fix" not "remediate".
3. Specific beats general, always. "Your contact page has no phone number" beats "your contact experience could be optimized". If a sentence could appear in any business's report, cut it.
4. Direct address: "you", "your site", "your buyers". Never "the client" or "one should".
5. Honest about trade-offs and timeframes. Saying "this will take 3–6 months to show results" builds more trust than any promise.
6. Confident, not salesy. State findings like a consultant who has seen this a hundred times. Never plead, never hype, never superlatives about Catalyst.
7. Numbers where they exist, silence where they don't. Never invent a statistic to sound authoritative.

## Banned vocabulary and patterns
- Words: leverage, elevate, empower, unlock, seamless, game-changing, cutting-edge, revolutionize, supercharge, robust, holistic, synergy, "in today's digital landscape", "in the ever-evolving world of".
- Patterns: rule-of-three adjective stacks ("fast, reliable, and scalable") · negative parallelism ("it's not just X, it's Y") · rhetorical questions as transitions · starting consecutive sentences with the same word · em-dash overuse · exclamation marks · ALL-CAPS emphasis.
- Any sentence whose only job is to praise Catalyst.

## Sentence-level examples
- Bad: "Your online presence presents significant opportunities for optimization across multiple touchpoints."
- Good: "Your site loads in 6 seconds on mobile. Most visitors leave at 3."
- Bad: "By leveraging cutting-edge AI solutions, you can revolutionize your lead generation."
- Good: "Right now, a lead who emails you at 8pm waits until morning for a reply. An auto-response with a booking link would catch some of those."
- Bad: "We recommend a comprehensive, holistic content strategy."
- Good: "Publish two articles a month aimed at the three questions your buyers actually search. Start there."

## Formatting in reports
Headings in plain sentence case. Short paragraphs (2–4 sentences). Bullets only for genuine lists (findings, quick wins), never for prose. One CTA line at the end, no buttons mid-report, no bold-spam.
