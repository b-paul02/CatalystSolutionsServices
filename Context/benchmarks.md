# Benchmark Library — the ONLY statistics the report may cite

Rules for the pipeline: the Synthesis node may attach a supporting statistic to a finding **only from this list**, quoted with its source exactly as written. If no benchmark fits, the finding stands on evidence from the pack alone. Never invent, round, or extrapolate a statistic. The Critic fails any stat not traceable to this file or the evidence pack.

Format: `id · stat text · source · use when`

## Speed & mobile
- SPD-1 · 53% of mobile visits are abandoned if a page takes longer than 3 seconds to load · Google/SOASTA mobile page speed research · site has slow mobile performance score or high LCP
- SPD-2 · As page load time goes from 1s to 3s, the probability of bounce increases 32% · Google mobile page speed benchmarks · moderately slow pages
- SPD-3 · The best-performing sites keep Largest Contentful Paint under 2.5 seconds — Google's own "good" threshold · Google Core Web Vitals documentation · LCP above 2.5s measured

## Tracking & analytics
- TRK-1 · Businesses that adopt data-driven marketing are roughly 6x more likely to be profitable year-over-year · Forbes/BCG data-driven marketing research · no analytics installed

## Forms & conversion
- FRM-1 · Reducing form fields from 11 to 4 has been shown to lift conversions by up to 120% · unbounce/HubSpot form studies · long forms found (5+ fields)
- FRM-3 · 78% of buyers purchase from the company that responds to their enquiry first · Lead Connect / speed-to-lead research · slow or manual enquiry follow-up

## Search & content
- SEO-1 · The first page of Google captures over 90% of search traffic; page two receives under 6% · Backlinko CTR studies · business not ranking for its key terms
- SEO-2 · 68% of online experiences begin with a search engine · BrightEdge research · low search visibility overall
- SEO-3 · Companies that blog consistently generate 67% more leads per month than those that don't · DemandMetric/HubSpot content research · no blog or stale blog
- SEO-4 · Organic search results take 3–6+ months to move meaningfully for most sites — month one is foundation, not rankings · consensus of SEO industry studies · any SEO route (also mandated by the estimate rubric)

## AI search
- AIS-2 · Pages with structured data are eligible for rich results, which earn measurably higher click-through rates than plain listings · Google Search Central case studies · no structured data found

## Local & reputation
- LOC-1 · 76% of people who search for something nearby on their smartphone visit a related business within a day · Google consumer insights · local business with weak local presence
- LOC-2 · 88% of consumers trust online reviews as much as personal recommendations · BrightLocal consumer review survey · few or no reviews

## Usage note
Every stat above has a named study behind it. If none fits the finding, the finding stands on the business's own evidence alone — never invent, soften, or approximate a statistic to fill the gap.
